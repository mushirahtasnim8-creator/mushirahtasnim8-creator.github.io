Projects

Spatiotemporal Distribution of Antibiotic Concentration and Their Association with Physiochemical Parameters of Buriganga River Water

This research project has been done during my M.Sc.where I investigated the presence and distribution of four antibiotic classes in one of the world's most severely polluted rivers using HPLC-MS/MS. The study examined correlations between water physicochemical characteristics and antibiotic concentrations to identify potential driving factors of contamination. Risk assessment methodology employing the Risk Quotient approach revealed significant ecological and human health hazards, alongside considerable antibiotic resistance risks. The study site's proximity to a major metropolitan hospital and the country's largest livestock market in the capital presented unique exposure pathways, making this a critical environmental and public health concern.

Assessment of Development Guide for Characterizing Oil Drill Cutting Stockpiles

This project involved a comprehensive characterization of an oil drill cuttings stockpile site to evaluate its environmental and physical properties. Environmental assessment included quantification of trace metals, semi-volatile organic compounds (SVOCs), volatile organic compounds (VOCs), and total petroleum hydrocarbons (TPH). Physical characterization was conducted using advanced instrumental techniques including X-ray fluorescence (XRF) for elemental composition, X-ray diffraction (XRD) for mineral phase identification, and thermogravimetric analysis (TGA) for thermal stability assessment. Incremental sampling methodology (ISM) was employed to ensure representative and statistically valid sampling across the stockpile site.
